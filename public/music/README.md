# Background Music

Place your background music file here as `background.mp3`.

## Requirements

- Format: MP3, Ogg, or WebM
- Duration: 3–10 minutes (will loop)
- Content: royalty-free Ukrainian folk music, instrumental

## Free sources

- https://freemusicarchive.org (filter: CC0 or Public Domain)
- https://musopen.org (classical, public domain)
- https://pixabay.com/music (free, no attribution needed)

## Recommended for this project

A warm, slow accordion or bandura instrumental works best.
Volume will be auto-ducked to ~20% when an announcement plays.
